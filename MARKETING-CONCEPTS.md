## Advertising and social concepts
Added a warm neutral Google/Meta advertising showcase followed by a matching warm neutral social-media showcase immediately after website projects. Sunday stems and Daybreak café are fictional concept brands; every preview is labelled. No campaign performance or client campaign claims are made. Enquiry links lead to the existing contact section. Images are local, optimized WebP assets and lazy loaded.
Platform marks: Simple Icons SVGs from https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons, stored in `public/brand/platforms/`.
Photography generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Final assets: `public/images/concept-florist.webp` and `public/images/concept-citrus.webp`.

Florist generation prompt:
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: photographic background for a bold florist advertising concept on an Australian web agency portfolio. Create one premium, photorealistic portrait 4:5 studio still life: a sculptural extravagant bouquet of vivid tangerine gerberas, coral anthuriums, pale peach roses and deep green leaves in a simple cherry-red cylindrical vase, on a soft warm pink seamless background and plinth. Punchy fashion editorial lighting, crisp petal texture, realistic shadows falling right, close art-directed composition. Bouquet occupies the lower two thirds, clean pink negative space in upper quarter for HTML headline. Strong coral, pink, orange and green contrast. This is a sample concept, no real florist identity. No text, no lettering, no logos, no borders, no interface, no watermarks.

Citrus generation prompt:
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: photographic background for a colourful social media content concept. One premium photoreal portrait 4:5 commercial studio photograph of a cold clear drinking glass filled with vivid orange juice, condensation, ice and one orange slice garnish, with two cut fresh oranges and green leaves at the base. Backdrop is rich chartreuse lime green seamless paper with hard sunlight and crisp interesting diagonal shadows. Glass centred in lower two thirds of frame, top quarter plain lime green negative space reserved for separate HTML typography. Playful high-end Australian cafe campaign art direction, macro surface detail, realistic glass and citrus, warm summer energy. No lettering, no text, no logos, no label, no UI or borders, no watermark.

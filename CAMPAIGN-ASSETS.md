## Automotive image prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium photographic image for a sample automotive service advertisement on an Australian web agency portfolio. Create one realistic landscape 4:3 photograph inside a tidy independent Australian mechanic workshop. Foreground: an adult mechanic in plain slate-grey workwear and protective gloves inspecting the engine bay of a modern silver unbranded car, bonnet open. Candid side angle, focused on skilled hands and the vehicle, person shown naturally from side rather than posing for camera. Workshop bay with organised tools and daylight through a roller door, softly out of focus. Honest local-business photography, believable mechanical details, bright natural lighting, warm stone, steel, muted olive and charcoal palette. No glamour, neon, excessive grime or cinematic dark grading. No text, no logos, no brands, no numbers, no watermark, no UI. The image will have HTML ad copy above it, so fill the frame with the useful photograph.

## Home services image prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: portrait 4:5 photograph for a sample home services social media campaign. A beautifully finished, believable Australian residential bathroom renovation: warm ivory large-format porcelain wall and floor tiles, oak floating vanity, understated brushed-nickel tapware, clear glass walk-in shower, soft daylight from a high window. Focus on precise tile alignment, material detail and clean workmanship. Simple practical modern room, not an enormous luxury mansion, no flowers, no plants, no overly decorated props. Photoreal editorial architectural photography, soft warm stone and muted sage accents, coherent geometry, natural shadows. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no UI. A finished-trades-work image that fits an understated web agency portfolio.

# Bluepeek search index plan

## Objective

Build a useful, internally connected search index that establishes Bluepeek as an Australian specialist in websites, local SEO, GEO and practical lead automation. Each page must answer a distinct customer need and contain original detail. We do not publish duplicated suburb pages or claims that cannot be verified.

## Information architecture

### Service hub

The national service pages explain the offer without relying on a city modifier:

- Website design
- Local SEO
- GEO optimisation
- Google Ads management
- Customer review systems
- Missed-call recovery
- AI phone agents
- AI chatbots
- Lead follow-up automation
- Lead-capture websites

### Location cluster

Gold Coast is the primary location entity. Perth remains a serviced market supported by real client work. Location pages must contain local proof, service detail or market context that is not repeated elsewhere.

Current priority pages:

- Website design Gold Coast
- AI automation Gold Coast
- Small-business websites Gold Coast
- Website design Perth
- AI automation Perth
- Small-business websites Perth

Future suburb pages require a relevant project, client example or genuinely local section before publication.

### Industry cluster

Industry pages connect Bluepeek's services to the buying journey of each customer type:

- Mechanics and automotive workshops
- Panel beaters
- Tradies
- Builders
- Electricians
- Plumbers
- Painters
- Barbers
- Beauty salons
- Clinics
- Cafes
- Restaurants

Each page links to the most relevant service and, when available, a real case study.

### Evidence cluster

Every completed client project should become a case study containing only confirmed information:

- Client, industry and location
- The initial requirement
- Work delivered
- Real screenshots
- Confirmed features
- Live website link
- Measured results only when evidence is available

### Editorial cluster

Publish decision-stage articles that support service pages rather than competing with them. Priority topics:

1. SEO vs Google Ads for a local service business
2. AI phone agent vs answering service
3. How missed-call text-back works
4. What local SEO costs in Australia
5. What GEO can and cannot do
6. Website redesign checklist for small businesses
7. Google Business Profile optimisation checklist
8. How to ask customers for genuine Google reviews

## Internal linking rules

- Every service page links to relevant industries, one location page and evidence where available.
- Every industry page links to website design, local SEO and the most relevant automation service.
- Every case study links to the service and industry pages it proves.
- Every article links to one primary commercial page and one supporting resource.
- Navigation and footer expose hubs; contextual links do the deeper work.

## Publishing standard

- One clear search intent per page.
- Original Australian English copy written for customers first.
- Accurate title, description, canonical URL and breadcrumb.
- Service, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data where valid.
- Factual claims only; no invented results, partnerships or guarantees.
- Mobile-first layout, accessible headings and descriptive links.
- Sitemap inclusion and crawlable server-rendered content.
- Review after 90 days using impressions, clicks, qualified enquiries and assisted conversions.

## Release sequence

### Release 1

Publish the national service hub and six additional industry pages, correct Gold Coast entity signals, expand structured data and update `llms.txt`.

### Release 2

Add supporting case studies and decision-stage guides based on verified client work and current offers.

### Release 3

Add location and suburb pages only where Bluepeek has local evidence or distinct market information, then strengthen external citations and links to the relevant landing pages.
